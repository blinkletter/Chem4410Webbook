27. **Hemiacetal formation**
    - The chemistry of hemiacetals is an important example in physical organic chemistry. This mechanism is essential in understanding polysaccharide formation and many other reactions in living systems.
28. **Imine formation**
    - The condensation of an amine with an aldehyde or ketone is a keystone reaction in organic chemistry. There is an enormous collection of work to choose from. 
29. **Acyl substitution**
    - Formation and cleavage of esters, amides, anhydrides and more involve this reaction. There are several mechanisms observed for this reaction. Which shall we choose as an example?
30. **Electrophilic addition to alkenes**
    - Bromination of an alkene was probably the first reaction mechanism that you encountered in your journey within organic chemistry. There are many examples of this reaction that are of importance to biochemistry and synthetic chemistry.

32. **E1 \& E2 eliminations**
    - Formation of alkenes vi elimination is another brick in the foundation of organic chemistry. We will explore some examples of this reaction using the methods of physical organic chemistry.
33. **Electrophilic aromatic substitution**
    - Aromaticity is still a mystery, depending on who you ask. This important electronic effect can be explored using this reaction as an example.
