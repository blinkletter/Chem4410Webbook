## The Deliverable
**Write** a clear and professional report describing **two** of these core concepts. **Choose** a subject using the table **above** and the **last digit** of your student number. In addition to that, **everyone** will be reporting on the Hammond postulate.  **Draw a reaction scheme** and a **potential energy diagram** with a **reaction coordinate** in each case and **explain** how it is consistent with the **idea** that you are presenting. Write **clearly** using the standards expressed in the **instructions** for assignment \#1. Can math **help you** prove your point? If so, use it.

Make a concise **document** with both the reaction scheme and the reaction coordinate diagram appropriately sized. Text should be single-spaced. **References** should be presented using an accepted style format (I prefer [ACS style](https://www.concordia.ca/library/guides/chemistry/acs.html) \[Web\].) Don’t forget a title, your name, a figure legend, etc. 

You may hand-draw the diagram but, when you photograph it, please **adjust** contrast and brightness so that the **lines are dark** and the paper is white. I cannot interpret shades of grey.  **I dare you** to draw your coordinate diagram by using a vector **graphics editor**. *Inkscape* is a free example and presentation programs like Microsoft *Powerpoint* also have drawing tools. 

The reaction scheme must be made using a **chemical drawing program** such as the editor on [**Chemicalize.com**](https://chemicalize.com/welcome) \[Web\].

Grading **written creative work** like this is always a **subjective** exercise. I don't deduct marks for errors, I give marks for **excellence**. Half of the grade will be for your **writing** and half will evaluate how neat, **clear and professional** you are in communicating your answer. Feel free to use a chemical drawing program and appropriate tools to generate plots and diagrams. Take pride in your work.

### Rubric

All the points in the **general rubric** presented in the instructions for assignment #1 apply. The document must have a **professional** level of quality for full marks.

The reaction scheme **must** relate to the reaction coordinate diagram. The written text should **define and explain** the principle and **convince** the reader that your scheme and diagram support that explanation.
