## During

We will review **Acid/base Equilibrium** and how to **calculate** the fraction of protonated/deprotonated species in a solution of a given acidity. We will make it super easy with the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation. What does $pK_a$ mean and how is it useful (even when the value seems ridiculous)? Then we will introduce **acidity Functions**. An acidity function is a way to express the strength of an acidic environment. $pH$ is just one example but it is only meaningful in dilute solution. What is the acidity of 90% sulfuric acid? The concept of $pH$, as defined by $-\log{[H^+]}$, is meaningless here. We will use the **Hammett acidity function**, $H_0$
