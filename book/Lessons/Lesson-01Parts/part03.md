# 3: Mechanisms and Arrow Pushing

On one side of a **reaction** equation, we have **atoms** connected by **bonds** and, on the other side, the **same** atoms connected by **different** bonds. How does do we get there from here?

## Learning Goals
After participating in the before, during and after of this class meeting you will have explored the following learning goals of lesson \#1&hellip;

- Draw a reaction **mechanism** using curved arrows that describes a **transition state** for each step of the reaction.
- Understand that the **curved arrows** are really just way to **describe** the transition state.
