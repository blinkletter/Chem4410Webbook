# Assignment \#8: Personal Exploration Proposal

The following assignment is to be **submitted** via moodle **before** the class meeting \#26.

## The Activity
This section describes the **activity** that you are to perform for the assignment. Do the following&hellip;

> **Choose a subject** that interests you. **Start reading** literature in the subject area and **choose a paper** that features your topic and presents data or other information that you can **analyze**. You will often find some information about your topic in our **textbook**. 

> **Write a one-page document** that describes the topic, gives the reference for the chosen paper, and explains you **initial plan** for your exploration (do you plan to replot any data, do you plan to drill deep into an aspect of the experimental method, do you plan to connect this paper to another, etc.) There are many directions one can take in a personal exploration. This is only a plan for **first steps**, not the map of the whole journey.

> **Prepare a Presentation** that summarizes your topic and plan. Only a single slide is permitted or you may use the whiteboard.


## The Deliverable
> After you have complete the assignment described above you will **submit your work**. 


As an example of what I am looking for, recall the plan document that I presented when we began our exploration of the nonclassical cation. You can find my plan on the [**class meeting page for the "Introduction to the Nonclassical cation"**](part24.md) page.

## Grading

Grading **written creative work** like this is always a **subjective** exercise. I don't deduct marks for errors, I give marks for **excellence**. Half of the grade will be for your **writing** and half will evaluate how neat, **clear and professional** you are in communicating your answer. Feel free to use a chemical drawing program and appropriate tools to generate plots and diagrams. Take pride in your work.

The plan must **include** a reference to the paper(s) that you plan to explore that can be **accessed** using the UPEI subscriptions. If you obtain the paper via interlibrary loan you must **include** it with your submission. I am looking for **evidence** that you have enough information to be able to anticipate a **successful** completion of the project.

### Rubric

All the points in the **general rubric** presented in the instructions for **assignment \#1** apply. The document must have a **professional** level of quality for full marks.

The report **must contain** all the elements described in the **instructions** above.

I will send you some **feedback** based on your plan. I may suggest lines of inquirey, point out opportunities and risks, and **give advice** about the scope of your plan.
