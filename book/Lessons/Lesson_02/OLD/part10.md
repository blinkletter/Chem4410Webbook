# 10: Bronsted Plots

The **acid** in an acid **catalyzed** reaction **transfers a proton** to the reactant. What if we **change the acid**? Let us imagine a whole series of acids with different $pK_a$ values. Would the **rate constant** for catalysis **change** in proportion to the $pK_a$ values? It might or it might not, depending on the **mechanism**. This will be our first example of using **structure-activity relationships** to interpret reaction mechansims. Today we introduce the **Brønsted plot**.

The $pK_a$ of an acid is a **function** of the acid dissociation constant, $K_a$. The value of $K_a$ ia a function of the **free-energy difference** for the proton transfer to water. If we plot $\log{k}$ *vs* $pK_a$ we are essential plotting the free-energy of activation against the free-energy of the proton transfer.
