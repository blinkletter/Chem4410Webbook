# 26: Personal Exploration Start

In this class meeting we will share our initial work on a **personal exploration**. Each student will give a **very brief presentation** on the subject they have chosen and the paper that they plan to explore. Everyone can change their minds later, but today we will express our **first steps**.
<hr>

## Before

**Choose a subject** that interests you. **Start reading** literature in the subject area and **choose a paper** that features your topic and presents data or other information that you can **analyze**. You will often find some information about your topic in our **textbook**.

**Write a one-page document** that describes the topic, gives the reference for the chosen paper, and explains you **initial plan** for your exploration (do you plan to replot any data, do you plan to drill deep into an aspect of the experimental method, do you plan to connect this paper to another, etc.) There are many tacks one can take in a personal exploration.

> The above instructions comprise most of **Assignment \#8**, the second **assignment** from lesson \#5, which is **due before class**.  So be sure to have uploaded your PDF file to moodle. See the **assignment \#8** page in **lesson \#5** for instructions. Bring a copy of your one-page **proposal** and be prepared to present **your plan** as described below.


<hr>

## During

Each student will **hand out** their one page proposal/plan (I can print them for you if you send them to me in advance) and **present** their plan using a single presentation slide or the whiteboard.

Recall the plan and one page document that I presented when we began our exploration of the nonclassical cation. You can find the plan and my single presentation slide on the [**class meeting page for the "Introduction to the Nonclassical cation"**](part24.md) page.


<hr>

## After

After this class meeting you will have **taken the first steps** to completing your literature exploration. Now **continue reading** your selected paper(s) and following references to access methods or details when needed. Never hesitate to **ask for help**. Start early and ask early. **Give yourself the time** to be excellent.

I will send you some **feedback** based on your plan. I may suggest lines of inquirey, point out opportunities and risks, and **give advice** about the scope of your plan.


<hr>

## Learning Goals
After participating in the before, during and after of this class meeting you will have explored the following learning goals of lesson \#5&hellip;

- Have futhered your experience in public speaking and oral scientific communication.
- Practiced condensing information in to a brief yet informative document.
